---
title: My Dog Bruno
---

My name is Nina and I live in a small town. I do not live alone. Bruno lives with me. Bruno is my dog. He is brown and white and has long ears. Bruno is four years old.

In the morning Bruno wakes up first. He comes into my room and lays his head on my bed. Then I know: it is time. I get up and get dressed. Bruno is waiting at the door. He already has his lead in his mouth.

We go to the park. The park is big and quiet. In the morning there are only a few people there. Bruno runs across the meadow and looks for his ball. I throw the ball, and he brings it back. We do that for twenty minutes. Afterwards Bruno is tired and I am awake.

At home Bruno gets his food. He eats very quickly. I drink a tea and eat a slice of bread with jam. At eight o'clock I drive to work. I work in a pharmacy. Bruno stays at home and sleeps on the sofa. The sofa is really for people, but Bruno does not always listen well.

In the afternoon I come back. Bruno is already standing at the window and waiting. He jumps and barks and is happy. We go outside once more. Today we meet Frau Berger. She has a dog too. Her dog is called Luna and is very small. Bruno and Luna play together in the grass. Frau Berger and I talk about the weather.

In the evening I cook pasta. Bruno lies next to the table and looks at me. He would like pasta too, but pasta is not good for dogs. He gets a carrot. He likes carrots as well.

Afterwards I watch a film. Bruno lies next to me on the sofa. He snores a little. That is not loud, that is cosy.

At the weekend we sometimes drive to the lake. Bruno loves water. He jumps straight in and swims. Then he comes back and shakes himself, and I am wet too. The people on the shore laugh.

Sometimes my friends ask, "Isn't a dog a lot of work?" Yes, a dog is work. I get up early, I go outside even in the rain, and I often clean the floor. But Bruno is my friend. He is always there, and he is always happy. That is enough.
