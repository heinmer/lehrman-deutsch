---
title: My First Day as an Intern
---

Last Monday I started my internship at a small bookshop. I was quite nervous, because I had never worked in a shop before.

The alarm went off at half past six. I got up straight away, had a shower and quickly drank a tea. I could not eat any breakfast, because I was too excited. I took the bus into the town centre and arrived twenty minutes early.

The manager is called Frau Lorenz. She shook my hand right away and showed me the whole shop. First she explained to me how the till works. Then I was allowed to unpack the new books and sort them onto the shelves. That took much longer than I had thought, because every book has its fixed place.

Only a few customers came during the morning. An elderly lady asked me whether we had a book about birds. I did not know the answer and had to fetch Frau Lorenz. Afterwards I looked at the sections closely, so that next time I can help on my own.

During the lunch break I ate with my colleagues in the small kitchen. They were very friendly and asked me a lot of questions about my studies. One colleague told me that she has been working here for twelve years.

In the afternoon I had to decorate the shop window again. I put the books about summer at the front.

At six o'clock I finished for the day. I was tired, but content. Frau Lorenz said that I should come back tomorrow.
