---
title: The Saturday Market
---

Every Saturday I go to the market. The market is on the big square in front of the church. It starts at eight o'clock in the morning.

I get up early. I drink a coffee and eat a slice of bread with cheese. Then I take my bag and set off. The walk takes ten minutes.

The market is loud. Many people are buying fruit and vegetables. The sellers call out the prices. A kilo of apples costs two euros. The children eat an ice cream and play between the stalls.

I always buy from Frau Krause. She has a small stall at the end of the row. Her vegetables are fresh, because she has a garden outside the town. Today I take tomatoes, potatoes and a lettuce. Frau Krause gives me a carrot as well, for my dog.

Next to the vegetables there are flowers. The flowers are colourful and smell very good. My wife likes tulips. I buy ten red tulips for her.

At the end I have another coffee. Next to the fountain stands a small cart. The man there makes good coffee. I sit down on a bench and watch the people. An old man plays the guitar and sings a song.

At eleven o'clock I go home. My bag is heavy, but I am content. At home my wife is cooking a soup. Vegetables from the market simply taste better.
