---
title: The First Driving Lesson
---

I have been taking my driving licence since my eighteenth birthday. First I had to attend twelve theory lessons. There we learned the road signs and practised a lot of questions on the computer.

Last week my first driving lesson finally came. My driving instructor is called Herr Brandt. He is about fifty years old and speaks very calmly. That was good, because I had great respect for the car.

Before we set off, he explained everything to me: the seat, the mirrors, the seatbelt and the pedals. Then I was allowed to start the engine. My hands on the steering wheel were quite damp.

We started in a quiet street on the edge of town. At the beginning I only drove slowly straight ahead. The engine stalled twice, because I let the clutch out too quickly. Herr Brandt just laughed and said that it happens to everyone.

After twenty minutes I was allowed to turn off for the first time. I had to indicate, change gear and watch out for pedestrians all at once. That was a lot at one time, but I managed it.

At the end we drove a stretch along a bigger road. There was much more traffic there, and a bus was driving behind me. I was very glad when we were standing in front of the driving school again.

Herr Brandt said that I had driven quite well for a first lesson. But I still need a lot of lessons, especially on the motorway and at night. My next driving lesson is on Tuesday. I am already looking forward to it, but I am a little nervous too.
