---
title: The First Snow
---

It is winter. Anna wakes up early. Outside it is still dark. She goes to the window and looks out.

Snow has fallen overnight. The street is white. The cars stand under a thick blanket. A child runs to school and laughs loudly.

Anna drinks a coffee in the kitchen. Then she puts on her warm coat. She takes her gloves and a scarf. Today she is walking to work.

She stops for a moment in front of the house. The snow falls softly on her hands. The town is completely still. Anna smiles and thinks: winter is finally here.
