---
title: The Trip to Hamburg
---

During the autumn holidays I travelled to Hamburg with my friend Nele. We live in a small village in Bavaria, so the trip was something special for us. On Friday morning we got up at six o'clock. My mother drove us to the station, because the first bus does not leave until later. On the train we ate rolls and looked out of the window.

The journey took almost seven hours. First we saw mountains, then nothing but flat fields. Nele said that the north looks quite different from home. In Hanover we had to change trains, and we nearly missed our connection. Luckily we made it.

We arrived in Hamburg in the afternoon. The main station was very big and loud. We dropped off our rucksacks at the youth hostel and set out again straight away. Our room was on the fourth floor and had a small window facing the courtyard. From there you could see the roofs of the city.

In the evening we walked down to the harbour. Large ships from all over the world lie there. A man told us that his ship had come from Brazil. We bought fish rolls and sat down by the water. It was raining lightly, but that did not bother us. Nele took a lot of photos for her parents.

On Saturday we wanted to go on a harbour cruise. Unfortunately it rained all morning, so we went to the museum first. There we saw old maps and models of ships. In the afternoon the weather got better, and we were able to go out on a small boat after all. The wind was cold, but the sun shone on the water. Afterwards we sat in a café and drank hot chocolate. Nele wrote a postcard to her grandmother.

On Sunday we got up early, because we wanted to see the fish market. The traders there shout very loudly, and plenty of people are already standing among the stalls at seven in the morning. We bought fruit and flowers and packed everything into our rucksacks. At midday we took the train back home. I was tired, but very happy.

Now I really want to go to Hamburg again. Next time we want to stay longer, because two days are simply too short. Perhaps we can go to the sea as well then.
