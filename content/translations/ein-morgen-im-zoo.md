---
title: A Morning at the Zoo
---

On Sunday, the König family goes to the zoo early. The parents are travelling with their children Mia and Paul. Mia is nine years old, and Paul is six. Both children love animals and have been waiting a long time for this day.

In front of the entrance, the parents buy four tickets. The children get a map with lots of colourful pictures. Mia wants to go to the elephants first. Paul would rather see the penguins, but today Mia is allowed to start.

The zoo is still quiet in the morning. There are only a few people on the paths, and the air is cool. At the elephants, a keeper is cleaning the large enclosure. Two elephants are eating hay, and a young animal is playing with a branch.

The keeper brings apples and carrots. She tells the children something about the animals. An elephant eats a great deal of vegetables, grass and hay every day. Paul looks at the large ears and asks lots of questions.

Then the family goes to the ape house. A small monkey is jumping from one rope to another there. An older monkey sits very quietly by the window and looks at the visitors. Mia laughs because he has almost the same face as her tired father.

At eleven o'clock, everyone is hungry. They sit down at a table beside a small pond. Their mother takes sandwiches, cucumber and juice out of the rucksack. Ducks are swimming on the water, but the family does not give them any bread.

After the meal, Paul looks for his blue cap. It is no longer on his head, and it is not in the rucksack either. The family walks back along the same path. The cap is lying on a bench in front of the ape house, and Paul is very happy.

Finally, they visit the penguins. The animals walk slowly over the rocks and then jump quickly into the water. Paul stands in front of the pool for a long time. On the drive home, both children sleep in the car and dream about their next visit.
