---
title: Moving House on Saturday
---

After two years in a small student residence, Marie is moving into her first flat of her own. It is on the fourth floor of an old building. Marie has rented a van for the move and labelled many boxes with coloured notes. Her brother Tim arrives at seven in the morning. Two friends plan to help carry the furniture later.

Shortly after arriving, Marie receives two pieces of bad news. One friend is ill, and the other friend's train is cancelled. There is also a notice on the lift because it has been broken since the previous evening. Marie looks at the many boxes in the van and becomes nervous. She and Tim cannot finish the move alone before evening.

At that moment, Mrs Aydin comes out of the ground-floor flat. She has seen the van and asks whether Marie is the new tenant. When she hears about the broken lift, she calls her adult son Cem. Then she gets a small trolley for heavy boxes from the cellar. Marie is surprised that a neighbour she does not know wants to help immediately.

The four of them form a chain on the stairs. Tim takes the boxes out of the van, and Mrs Aydin carries them to the first floor. Cem carries them farther upstairs while Marie puts everything in the correct rooms. After an hour, heavy rain begins. They put old blankets over the furniture and continue working anyway.

The biggest problem is the wide sofa. It does not fit through the door on the third floor although Tim changes direction several times. Cem discovers that the four wooden feet can be unscrewed. Mrs Aydin brings a suitable spanner. Without the feet, they turn the sofa upright and slowly get it into Marie's living room.

Afterwards Marie wants to assemble her bookcase, but the bag of screws is missing. The hardware shop has already closed on Saturday afternoon. Tim searches the van again but finds only empty blankets there. Finally, Mrs Aydin opens a large jar of old screws. Three of them fit perfectly, and a few minutes later the bookcase is standing safely against the wall.

At around two o'clock, everyone is hungry and tired. Marie orders a large pizza and puts drinks on a moving box. Two more neighbours also stop by briefly and introduce themselves. One brings plates, and the other brings a warm apple cake. In this way, the short break becomes a small meal among the unopened boxes.

In the afternoon, the box with the coffee machine and all the cups suddenly goes missing. Marie is certain that she marked it with a red note, but it is not in the van. Cem eventually finds the box outside the wrong cellar door. Someone put it there during the rain. Soon the new flat smells of coffee, and everyone has energy again.

In the early evening, they carry the last lamp upstairs. Marie sweeps out the empty van and returns it to the rental company on time. On the way back, she gives the key to the student residence to the caretaker. Leaving makes her a little sad because many friends lived there. At the same time, she is looking forward to her new home and the larger kitchen.

When Marie returns, the lamp is already shining above the sofa. Tim has also assembled the bed, and Mrs Aydin puts a green plant on the windowsill. Marie thanks everyone with the last pieces of apple cake. There are still many boxes in the hallway, but that does not bother her. After this long Saturday, the old building already feels like home.
