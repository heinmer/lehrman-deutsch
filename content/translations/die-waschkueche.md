---
title: The Laundry Room in the Cellar
---

Nobody in our building has a washing machine in the flat. All the tenants do their washing downstairs in the cellar. There are two machines and a dryer down there.

A schedule hangs on the door. Every flat has one fixed day of the week. We were given Thursday, because the family on the first floor has had Wednesday for a long time.

Last week there was a problem. On Thursday morning the laundry room was occupied. The student from the attic flat had put his washing into both machines, even though Thursday is not his day.

At first I was really annoyed. But I did not want any quarrel in the building, so I waited. In the evening I went upstairs and rang his bell.

He apologised straight away. He said that he had not read the schedule properly, because he has only been living here for a month. Then he offered me his Saturday.

Now I do my washing on Saturday. That is even better for me, because on Thursday I work until eight in the evening. So sometimes a little trouble brings an advantage.
