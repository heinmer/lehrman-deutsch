---
title: The Dentist's Appointment
---

On Friday morning I had an appointment at the dentist's. For a week one of my teeth had been hurting, especially when I ate. So on Monday I rang the practice.

Three people were already sitting in the waiting room. I picked up a magazine, but I could not really read, because I was nervous. After twenty minutes a member of staff called my name.

The dentist had a close look at the tooth. "That is only a small hole," he said. "We'll do it right away." The treatment took ten minutes and did not hurt at all.

Afterwards I was not allowed to eat anything for two hours. On the way home I thought: from now on I'll brush my teeth better. In September I have to come back for a check-up.
