---
title: At the Supermarket
---

Herr Klein goes to the supermarket in the morning. He takes a trolley and a note out of his bag. On the note are bread, milk, eggs and cheese.

First he goes to the shelf with the bread. The bread is still warm. Then he looks for the milk. It is right at the back of the chilled shelves. The eggs he finds straight away.

A young woman is waiting at the till. She smiles and says, "Good morning!" Herr Klein pays by card. Then he packs everything into his bag.

Outside the sun is shining. Herr Klein walks home slowly. This evening he is cooking for his family.
