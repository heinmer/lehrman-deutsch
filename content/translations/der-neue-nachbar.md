---
title: The New Neighbour
---

A new neighbour has been living in our building for a week. His name is Herr Özdemir and he comes from Turkey. He moved in on Monday. All day long men carried furniture up to the third floor.

On Tuesday I met him for the first time. We were standing together in the lift. He greeted me warmly and said that he is pleased with the new flat. His German is not perfect yet, but I understood everything.

Herr Özdemir works as a cook in a restaurant in town. He often has to work until late at night, so he sleeps in in the mornings. At the weekend he is off.

Yesterday he rang our doorbell. He had a plate of cake with him, because he wanted to introduce himself. My mother invited him in, and we drank tea together. He told us about his family. His wife and his children are only coming to Germany in the autumn, because the children are still at school there.

I think we have got a good neighbour. Next week he wants to invite us to his restaurant.
