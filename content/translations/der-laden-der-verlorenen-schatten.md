---
title: The Shop of Lost Shadows
---

Emir comes home late from his guitar lesson every Tuesday evening. His route leads through a street with a bakery, a pharmacy and many old street lamps. After an autumn storm, hardly anyone is outside. Emir walks quickly because cold rain is falling on his jacket. Beneath the last lamp, he suddenly notices that his shadow is no longer with him.

Emir turns around and sees the shadow still standing beneath the previous lamp. He raises an arm, but the dark figure does not move. Then the shadow runs into a narrow alley without Emir. Emir follows it and calls out, but of course he receives no answer. At the end of the alley, a door with a violet light slowly opens.

Behind the door is a small shop that Emir has never seen before. Tall shelves with round jars stand along the walls. A different shadow moves inside every jar. Some look like people, while others look like dogs, birds or bicycles. An older woman with silver hair sits behind a long table and sorts black cards.

The woman's name is Mrs Dämmer, and she collects lost shadows. She explains that the strong storm separated many shadows from their owners. Normally they find their way back alone as soon as the sun shines again. Some of them, however, became lost in the dark streets. If they do not return home before sunrise, they slowly forget their old shapes.

At first Emir only wants to look for his own shadow. Mrs Dämmer places twelve jars on the table and opens their lids. The shadows jump out and wait on the floor. Emir acts out several movements from his guitar lesson. Eleven shadows do something different, but the twelfth holds its arms exactly as Emir does for a difficult chord.

Before Emir can touch it, a very small shadow pulls at his trouser leg. It clearly belongs to a child and trembles with fear. Mrs Dämmer has no card for it because the wind carried the card out of the window. The little shadow repeatedly shows a round window and a bridge. Emir knows a house with windows like that on the other side of the canal.

Emir leaves his guitar in the shop and carries the child's shadow inside a lantern. His own shadow walks beside him willingly. The rain has stopped, but many branches are lying in the streets. At the canal bridge, the little shadow suddenly becomes very excited. A light is shining in a round window in a house beyond it.

A tired mother opens the door and leads Emir into the child's room. Her son Sami is sitting awake in bed although it is already very late. Since the storm, he has been afraid of every lamp because no shadow appears beside him. Emir opens the lantern, and the little figure immediately jumps to Sami's feet. For the first time that evening, the boy smiles.

When Emir returns to the alley, Mrs Dämmer is already waiting outside the shop. His own shadow stands behind him and joins itself to his shoes again. Mrs Dämmer says that a shadow never forgets kindness. She gives Emir a black card without any writing. A silver door only appears on it when a shadow somewhere needs help.

The next morning Emir goes back to the alley. There is only a grey wall between the bakery and the pharmacy. He finds no door and no violet light. The sun finally shines on his way to school, and his shadow walks beside him again. At the canal bridge, the dark figure stops for a moment and waves up at a round window.
