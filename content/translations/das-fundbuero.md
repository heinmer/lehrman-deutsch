---
title: The Lost Property Office
---

Two weeks ago I left my rucksack on the tram. I only noticed when I was already standing at my front door, searching every pocket for my key. Luckily my sister lives in the same building, otherwise I would have spent the night on the staircase.

The next morning I went to the lost property office. It is in the basement of an old administrative building, and you have to ring the bell before you are allowed in. Behind the counter sat a woman who was just drinking a coffee and gave me a friendly look. I had to fill in a form: When? Which line? What colour? I could hardly remember whether the tram had been going towards the main station or towards the zoo.

While she searched on the computer, I had a look around. On the shelves stood umbrellas, cycle helmets, glasses, a cello and even a pram. The woman told me that most of the things are never collected, because a lot of people have no idea that the lost property office even exists.

My rucksack was there. Somebody had handed it in that same evening without leaving their name, and nothing was missing, not even my wallet. Ever since, whenever I get off the tram I think of that unknown person – and take a look at the seat next to me.
