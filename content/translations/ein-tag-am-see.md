---
title: A Day at the Lake
---

It is summer. Lena and Tom ride their bicycles to the lake. The way is long, but beautiful. Tall trees stand to the left and right.

After an hour they arrive at the lake. The water is calm and blue. Many families are sitting on the meadow. A dog runs into the water and barks loudly.

Lena spreads a blanket on the grass. Tom takes the food out of the rucksack. They have rolls, apples and a bottle of water with them. They eat and talk about school.

In the afternoon they swim in the lake. The water is cold, but pleasant. Afterwards they lie in the sun and read. Tom almost falls asleep.

In the evening they ride back. The sky is red and the air is warm. Lena says, "That was a lovely day." Tom nods and laughs.
