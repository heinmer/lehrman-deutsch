---
title: A Rainy Day
---

It is raining today in the city. The sky is grey and the clouds hang low over the houses. The streets are wet and shine in the light of the streetlamps. I stand at the window and look outside for a long time.

On the radio the weatherman talks about the rain. He says: The rain stays all day. In the evening it might be dry again. I listen to the news and drink my coffee.

I put on a warm jacket and take my umbrella. My shoes are old, but they are waterproof. I pack my book and my glasses in the bag. Then I leave the house and walk to the bus stop.

Many people are already waiting at the stop. A man reads a newspaper under his big umbrella. A woman wears a yellow raincoat and blue boots. Two children jump happily in the puddles. They laugh loudly and have wet feet. Their mother calls: Come now, the bus is here!

The bus comes late, because the street is full. I get on and sit by the window. The windows are a little foggy and cold. I draw a heart on the glass with my finger. Next to me sits an old woman with a basket full of apples.

In the city I go to the café on the market square first. The café is warm, bright and cosy. There I meet my friend Nina. We order two hot cocoas and a piece of apple cake. The cocoa tastes sweet and the cake is still warm. We sit at a table by the window and watch the rain. Nina talks about her work and I talk about my family.

After the café I go to the library alone. It is very quiet there and nobody speaks loudly. I look for a new book for my daughter. She loves stories about animals, especially about bears and wolves. I find a nice book about a little bear and take it with me right away. At the checkout the librarian shows me a second book about birds as well.

In the afternoon the rain slowly stops. The sun comes out briefly between the clouds. The air smells fresh and clean. Many people open their windows and air out the flat. The birds sing loudly again in the trees.

I walk home, because the way is not far. On the way I see a colourful rainbow over the roofs. I stop for a moment and take a photo for my daughter. A neighbour waves at me in a friendly way and asks about tomorrow's weather.

At home I hang up my wet jacket right away. My daughter runs towards me and is very happy about the new book. We sit down together on the sofa and open the first page. I read her the story of the little bear. Outside the sky slowly turns blue again and the last clouds disappear. In the evening we cook a warm soup together and set the table.
