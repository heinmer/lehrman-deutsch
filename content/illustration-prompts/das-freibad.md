Create a wide horizontal 2D storybook illustration for a German reading practice website.

SCENE DESCRIPTION:
Early on a summer morning at a small-town open-air swimming pool from the nineteen-fifties. A single swimmer moves down the length of a long rectangular concrete pool, placed slightly off-centre, leaving a quiet trail across the flat blue water. To the right, two volunteers — an older man with grey hair and a stout woman in an apron — are opening a low wooden kiosk and setting out folding chairs on a grass lawn with a few empty deckchairs. To the left stand simple wooden changing huts and a strip of dark green woodland that runs off the edge of the frame; on the far right, beyond a low fence, a flat sports ground with goalposts extends the horizon. The light is cool and low, with long soft shadows across the empty lawn, and the mood is calm and unhurried.

VISUAL STYLE:

A simple, charming, hand-drawn children's storybook illustration with a warm European picture-book aesthetic.

The image should feel like a traditional illustrated storybook, but translated into a clean modern 2D digital illustration.

Use a FLAT, GRAPHIC, SIMPLIFIED illustration style.

The artwork should be intentionally simple and visually economical. Use large, clearly defined shapes and broad areas of color rather than intricate details.

Think of a beautifully illustrated children's book with a slightly naive, handmade quality.

Use:
- simple organic shapes
- soft hand-drawn contours
- slightly imperfect edges
- broad flat color areas
- minimal shading
- subtle paper or painted texture
- simple expressive characters
- decorative but uncomplicated backgrounds
- a limited harmonious color palette

The illustration should look DRAWN and PAINTED, not rendered.

Avoid realism completely.

Do not create a highly detailed digital painting.
Do not create realistic textures.
Do not create cinematic photorealistic lighting.
Do not create complex atmospheric perspective.
Do not create highly detailed environments.

SIMPLICITY IS IMPORTANT.

Use only enough visual detail to communicate the scene clearly.

CHARACTERS:

If characters are present, make them charming, simple and expressive.

Use simplified anatomy and recognizable silhouettes.

Faces should be minimal and expressive, with simple eyes, noses and mouths.

Clothing should be represented with simple shapes and a few characteristic details rather than realistic folds and fabric texture.

Characters should feel like hand-drawn storybook figures rather than modern cartoon or 3D animation characters.

ENVIRONMENT:

Keep the environment simple and stylized.

Represent trees, houses, streets, snow, furniture, plants and other objects using large simplified shapes.

Do not fill the background with small decorative details.

Use overlapping shapes and a few carefully chosen elements to establish the location.

The background should support the story without competing with the main subject.

COLOR:

Use a limited, muted, harmonious palette.

Prefer warm earthy colors, dusty greens, muted blues, warm ochre, terracotta, cream, soft brown and subdued yellow.

Colors should feel slightly vintage and printed, with a gentle softness.

Avoid neon colors, highly saturated colors, glossy gradients and excessive color variation.

LIGHTING AND SHADING:

Keep lighting extremely simple.

Use mostly flat colors with only subtle tonal variation.

Avoid dramatic cinematic lighting.

Avoid realistic shadows, reflections, glow effects, volumetric light and complex gradients.

Any shadows should be simple, soft graphic shapes.

TEXTURE:

Add a very subtle handmade paper / paint texture.

The texture should be delicate and understated.

It should suggest traditional printed children's-book artwork without becoming visibly grainy or photorealistic.

The image should remain clean and graphic.

LINEWORK:

Use soft, organic, hand-drawn outlines where appropriate.

Lines should not be perfectly geometric or mechanically precise.

Avoid heavy black comic-book outlines.

Use dark muted colors for outlines rather than pure black whenever possible.

COMPOSITION:

Create a very wide panoramic composition designed specifically for a website hero banner.

Aspect ratio: approximately 3:1.

Use a simple, readable horizontal composition.

Place the main character or main narrative element slightly off-center when appropriate.

Allow the environment to extend naturally across the entire width.

Use large areas of calm negative space.

The composition should remain readable even when viewed at a small size.

Keep important characters and objects inside a central safe area so the image can be cropped responsively on different screens.

Do not overcrowd the frame.

The illustration should look like a SINGLE LARGE STORYBOOK SPREAD rather than a collection of many small elements.

OUTPUT:

Flexible high-resolution panoramic format.

Preferred aspect ratio: 3:1.

High resolution, generator-dependent.

The composition must be crop-safe and remain visually balanced if slightly cropped from the left or right.

NO TEXT:

Do not include any written words, letters, captions, subtitles, labels, logos, watermarks or typography anywhere in the image.

OVERALL IMPRESSION:

Simple.
Warm.
Hand-drawn.
Flat.
Graphic.
Charming.
Storybook-like.
Slightly nostalgic.
European children's book aesthetic.

Prioritize SIMPLE SHAPES, CLEAR SILHOUETTES, FLAT COLORS and EXPRESSIVE STORYTELLING over detail and realism.
